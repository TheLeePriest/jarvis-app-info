Jarvis Second Brain

A private personal assistant for its owner’s own notes, tasks and authorised email accounts. There is no public signup service.

Email support

With account-owner consent, Jarvis reads recent Gmail messages to prepare concise summaries, suggest replies or next actions, and identify dates stated in messages. It reads sent messages to avoid mistaking an already answered conversation for an unanswered one.

The email connection requests read-only Gmail access. It does not send, delete, archive or mark messages as read. Suggested tasks require the owner’s acceptance.
