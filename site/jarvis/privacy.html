Privacy and data handling

Jarvis Second Brain is a private assistant operated by its owner for personal use. This notice describes the email-summary connection.

Access and purpose

The connection reads messages, sender and recipient details, subjects, dates, labels and thread identifiers from Google accounts that the owner expressly authorises. It uses recent message excerpts to prepare email summaries and suggested next actions, and sent-message context to identify existing replies. It does not request permission to send or change mail.

Processing and storage

The connector runs on the owner’s server. Authentication credentials are stored separately from the notes and are not given to the conversational AI. A bounded, rolling snapshot of message excerpts is made available to the assistant. Attachments are not downloaded. Recognised authentication-code and credential messages are omitted from excerpts, although automated detection is not infallible.

Selected email excerpts are sent to the configured AI provider, OpenAI, to generate the requested summaries. Results are delivered to the owner’s private Telegram conversation. Working excerpts and accepted commitments may be stored in the owner’s Obsidian vault and synchronised using encrypted Obsidian Sync. These services process data under their applicable terms and account settings.

Retention and control

The working snapshot is replaced as it refreshes. Conversation histories, accepted task records, sync version history and backups may retain earlier material. Disconnecting an account stops future reads but does not automatically erase existing notes or histories.

The owner can revoke Jarvis Second Brain’s access in Google Account settings, stop the connector and delete saved excerpts and histories using the relevant services. Email data is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally supplied to train a general-purpose model by this connector.

Scope

This application has no public account-registration service and is intended only for its owner’s authorised accounts. This notice does not change the terms of Google, OpenAI, Telegram or Obsidian.
